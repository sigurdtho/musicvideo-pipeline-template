## Scheme

Target BPM, key, and genre.

Words per second = words in the section ÷ (bars × 4 × 60 ÷ BPM).

In a slow genre, verses stay at or under about 2.3 words/s. A sung chorus may sit higher. A spoken line that only fits if the voice rushes is rewritten before the first take.
